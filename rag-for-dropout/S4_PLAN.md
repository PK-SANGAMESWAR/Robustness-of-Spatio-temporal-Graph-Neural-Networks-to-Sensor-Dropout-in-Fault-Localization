## 4. The evidence decision (your instruction)
tau (the top-1 similarity threshold, fitted on dev only) is **one input**, not the decision. The generator must also return `evidence_status` ("sufficient" or "insufficient") and a reason in its structured output (`prompts/generator_v0.md`). The final decision is fail-closed:
**insufficient evidence if tau flags it OR the generator says "insufficient"**; the report then states that the evidence is insufficient and gives no procedural statement.
Deterministic tests (no LLM judge; run before any score is reported):
1. a unit test of the decision function with all four combinations of (tau flag, generator status), with a mocked generator;
2. the schema test: `evidence_status` is present and is one of the two values; if "insufficient", the report has no procedural claim (checked by the schema and the citation validator);
3. a run of `prompts/evidence_check_v0.md` on every golden retrieval query: the fraction of the 8 reviewed unanswerable queries (and, after your review, the 12 near-miss queries) with a final "insufficient" decision, and the fraction of answerable queries that are wrongly flagged.
The tau-only numbers (dev, tau 0.464 for v2: unanswerable 4 of 4 flagged, answerable 5 of 22 flagged on test) are the baseline that the combined decision must beat; no threshold is chosen from the test split.
