# Fault types and fault resistances

## Fault types
There are 11 fault types:

- line-to-ground (LG): AG, BG, CG
- line-to-line (LL): AB, BC, CA
- double line-to-ground (LLG): ABG, BCG, CAG
- three-phase: ABC, ABCG

The letters are the faulted phases; a trailing G means the fault includes ground (ABG, ABCG). ABC is a three-phase fault without ground.

## Fault resistance
Three fault resistances are simulated: 0.1, 1.0, 10.0 Ω. The 10 Ω faults draw the least current and are the hardest to localize.
