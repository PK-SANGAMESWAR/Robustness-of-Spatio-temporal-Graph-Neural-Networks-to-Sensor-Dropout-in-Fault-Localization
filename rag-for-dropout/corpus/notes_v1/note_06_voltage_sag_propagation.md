# How a voltage sag spreads along a feeder

During a fault the voltage is lowest at the fault point and recovers with electrical distance from it. The depth of the sag at a sensor depends on the impedance between the sensor and the fault compared with the impedance between the fault and the source. Sensors close to the fault therefore carry the most information about its location. This is why losing the sensors nearest to a fault (targeted dropout) hurts more than losing random sensors.
