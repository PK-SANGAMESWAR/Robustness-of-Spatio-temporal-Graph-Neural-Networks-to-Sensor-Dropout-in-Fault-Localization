# Single line-to-ground faults

A single line-to-ground (LG) fault connects one phase conductor to ground through a fault impedance. In symmetrical components the positive-, negative- and zero-sequence networks are connected in series at the fault point, so the fault current has all three sequence components. At the fault the voltage of the faulted phase falls toward zero when the fault resistance is low, while the voltages of the other two phases change much less. In this project the LG fault types are AG, BG and CG.
