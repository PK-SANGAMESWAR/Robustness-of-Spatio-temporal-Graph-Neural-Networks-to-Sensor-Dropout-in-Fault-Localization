# Line-to-line faults

A line-to-line (LL) fault connects two phase conductors without a ground connection. Only the positive- and negative-sequence networks take part, connected in parallel at the fault point, and no zero-sequence current flows. The voltages of the two faulted phases move toward each other. In this project the LL fault types are AB, BC and CA.
