# S1.2 — L-grid step decision (Gate 1 input)

**Status: LOCKED 2026-09-25. Selected L step = 0.05 (17 points).**

Per `03_STAGE_B_operator.md` S1.2, this selection is **not automatic** — the script reports the
distributions, a human chooses. Recorded here so the reasoning survives independently of
`operator/grid.json`'s machine-written fields.

## Evidence

- `results/pcstgnn/operator/lgrid/lgrid_summary.json` — full ρ-margin distributions at the
  pre-registered n=3000 stratified sample (seed 20260923), all 3 candidate steps (0.1, 0.05,
  0.025) × 4 σ_ε values (the fixed noise grid {1e-3, 5e-3, 1e-2} p.u. plus each step's own
  discretisation σ), against the 0.0125-step reference.
- `results/pcstgnn/operator/lgrid/tap_discontinuities.json` — 63 circuit-wide regulator-tap
  boundaries in μ(L) over L ∈ [0.5, 1.3], jumps up to 1.4e-2 p.u., present in the no-fault row too.

## Reasoning

At the physically relevant fixed σ (Paper 1's noise grid: 1e-3 / 5e-3 / 1e-2 p.u.), the median
ρ-margin and the saturation fraction (frac > 10 nat) are **essentially step-invariant** across
all three candidates — e.g. median margin at σ=5e-3: 44.75 / 44.72 / 44.40 nats for steps
0.1 / 0.05 / 0.025 respectively, agreeing to under 1%. Grid density barely matters once real
measurement noise dominates the denominator.

The only place the candidates diverge meaningfully is each step's own discretisation-σ row:
finer grids give a *smaller* numerical floor (5.768e-3 → 5.145e-3 → 3.551e-3 p.u.), which is
good — it keeps σ_ε subordinate to real σ₀ rather than an artifact of the table — but a *wider,
more saturating* margin at that self-consistent σ (61.4% > 10 nat at 0.1, 62.6% at 0.05, 65.7%
at 0.025). Step 0.05 sits in the middle on both axes: a meaningfully smaller numerical floor than
0.1 without 0.025's larger saturation increase, at no additional cost — the table was already
built (70,142 solves, 0 non-converged, 0 NaN).

**This was not selected on interpolation fidelity.** The original "coarsest step with
interpolation error < 1e-3 p.u." criterion is unreachable at any of the three candidates — p99
error only falls from 5.77e-3 → 5.15e-3 → 3.55e-3 p.u., an order of magnitude above 1e-3, because
it is capped by the tap discontinuities in μ(L), not by grid resolution. Refining the grid
further would not close that gap; it would only continue trading numerical floor for saturation
along the pattern above. `01_SPEC.md` and `03_STAGE_B_operator.md`'s superseded fidelity-based
rule are left as written — this note documents why the *current* S1.2 rule (ρ dynamic range) was
the one applied, without editing the frozen spec to make 0.05 look pre-decided.
